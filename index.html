About Chinvano Club

Chinvano Club is a dedicated group of 12 farmers under the Tigwepo Main Club in Lusaka. We foster sustainable farming, smart trading, and investment in agriculture. Our mission is to provide fresh, quality farm products while uplifting our community.

We are proud to showcase our hard work at the July 4th Farm Show — come meet us and taste the difference.
